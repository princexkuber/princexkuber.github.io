👤 About Kuber Singh Kushwaha
Kuber Singh Kushwaha, affectionately known as Prince, is a dedicated scholar, student leader, orator, and creative writer from Jharia, Dhanbad, Jharkhand. Currently serving as the Head Boy (2026–27) at Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Senior Secondary School, Digwadih, this portfolio reflects every facet of his academic journey, leadership, literary voice, and creative endeavors.
  • Academic Stream & Institution: Enrolled in Class 11 (Science Stream — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics alongside Computer Science and Commercial Art) at Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Senior Secondary School, Digwadih, Dhanbad.
  • Voice, Solo Singing & Public Speaking: A passionate solo vocalist and confident public speaker who leads school assemblies and captivates audiences with clarity, conviction, and poise.
  • Hindi Literature & Self-Written Poetry: Deeply connected to Hindi language and literature, Kuber composes his own original, self-written Hindi poems (Hindi Kavita) exploring human values, inspiration, and culture.
  • History, Philosophy & Multilingual Exploration: An avid reader of Indian and world history, philosophical ideas, and multiple languages, continuously broadening his intellectual horizon.
  • Digital Content Creation: An independent digital content creator sharing thoughtful reflections, storytelling, and student leadership insights across platforms.
🏆 Achievements & Honors
  • INTERNATIONAL LEVEL

    International Hindi Olympiad: Represented at the international level in the International Hindi Olympiad, demonstrating exceptional mastery over Hindi literature, grammar, and expression among global participants.
  • NATIONAL REWARD • ALL INDIA RANK 4

    All-India Computer Olympiad (Bloom Computer Olympiad): Secured National Rank 4 across India in a highly competitive national-level examination testing advanced logical reasoning, algorithmic thinking, and computer science fundamentals.
  • ZONAL LEVEL COMPETITION REWARD

    Mind Martians — Dhanbad Zonal Level Quiz Competition: Selected and rewarded at the prestigious Mind Martians Dhanbad Zonal Level Quiz Competition, representing the institution against top student teams across the district.
  • EXTRAORDINARY TALENT

    Kavi Sammelan Participation & Poetic Excellence: Showcased extraordinary literary talent across 3 Kavi Sammelans, earning wide acclaim for expressive poetic recitation and literary presence.
  • Anchoring & Stage Handling Leadership: Successfully anchored and managed the stage for major school assemblies, cultural programs, and literary events—coordinating live proceedings, guest introductions, and audience engagement with confidence and discipline.
  • Original Self-Written Poetry: Authored a collection of own-written Hindi poems reflecting social awareness, youth motivation, and philosophical depth.
  • Inter-House Debate Competitions: Demonstrated persuasive articulation and analytical debate skills across 2 Inter-House Debate Competitions at the school level.
📘 Academics & Leadership Journey
  • Class 11 (2026–27) — Currently Head Boy: Entrusted with the highest student leadership office as Head Boy at Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Senior Secondary School, carrying major institutional responsibilities including student council governance, school discipline, assembly leadership, and representing the student body.
  • Class 10 — Class Monitor: Served as Class Monitor across two terms, ensuring classroom coordination, peer discipline, and seamless communication between students and faculty.
  • Class 9 — Academic Captain: Appointed Academic Captain, mentoring classmates, organizing peer study initiatives, and fostering a disciplined academic environment.
  • Class 8 — School Leader: Recognized as a School Leader in his previous institution, actively leading school assemblies, house activities, and student welfare initiatives.
