👤 About Kuber Singh Kushwaha
Kuber Singh Kushwaha, affectionately known as Prince, is a dedicated scholar, student leader, orator, and creative thinker from Jharia, Dhanbad, Jharkhand. Currently serving as the Head Boy (2026–27) at Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Senior Secondary School, Digwadih, he balances analytical rigor in Science with deep artistic and literary expression.
  • Academic Stream & Discipline: Enrolled in Class 11 (Science Stream — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics alongside Computer Science and Commercial Art) at Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Senior Secondary School, Digwadih, Dhanbad.
  • Voice, Solo Singing & Stage Presence: A passionate solo vocalist and confident public speaker who regularly leads morning assemblies, anchors major cultural events, and captivates audiences with clarity and poise.
  • Hindi Literature & Poetry Writing: Deeply rooted in Hindi language and literature, Kuber composes original Hindi poetry (Hindi Kavita) and actively participates in literary gatherings and Kavi Sammelans.
  • History, Philosophy & Multilingual Curiosity: An avid reader of Indian and world history, philosophical thought, and diverse languages, always seeking deeper meaning behind human ideas and culture.
  • Digital Content Creation: Founder and creator of Kuber's Circle, crafting thoughtful, reflective, and educational digital content that inspires peers and young learners.
🏆 Achievements & Honors
  • INTERNATIONAL LEVEL

    International Hindi Olympiad: Represented at the international level in the International Hindi Olympiad, showcasing exceptional command over Hindi grammar, vocabulary, and literary comprehension among global participants.
  • NATIONAL REWARD • ALL INDIA RANK 4

    All-India Computer Olympiad (Bloom Computer Olympiad): Secured National Rank 4 across India in a highly competitive national-level examination testing advanced logical reasoning, algorithmic thinking, and computer fundamentals—a milestone achieved through rigorous preparation.
  • Mind Martians — Dhanbad Zonal Level Quiz Competition: Selected to represent the school at the prestigious Mind Martians Dhanbad Zonal Level Quiz Competition, competing against top student teams across the district in general knowledge and analytical quizzing.
  • Kavi Sammelan Anchoring & Stage Leadership: Hosted and anchored 3 school-level Kavi Sammelans, earning wide appreciation from faculty and guests for eloquent stage management and poetic presentation.
  • Original Poetry & Literary Recitation: Recognized across school literary forums for composing and reciting original Hindi poems reflecting social awareness, youth inspiration, and cultural heritage.
  • Inter-House Debate Competitions: Demonstrated persuasive articulation and critical thinking across 2 Inter-House Debate Competitions at the institutional level.
📘 Academics & Leadership Journey
  • Class 11 (2026–27) — Currently Head Boy: Entrusted with the highest student leadership office as Head Boy at Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Senior Secondary School, carrying major institutional responsibilities including student council governance, school discipline, assembly leadership, and representing the student body.
  • Class 10 — Class Monitor: Served as Class Monitor across two terms, ensuring classroom coordination, peer discipline, and seamless communication between students and faculty.
  • Class 9 — Academic Captain: Appointed Academic Captain, mentoring classmates, organizing peer study initiatives, and fostering a disciplined academic environment.
  • Class 8 — School Leader: Recognized as a School Leader in his previous institution, actively leading school assemblies, house activities, and student welfare initiatives.
  • Class 7 — Grade A Scholar: Achieved Grade A across all subjects, establishing a strong foundation of academic excellence and consistency.
